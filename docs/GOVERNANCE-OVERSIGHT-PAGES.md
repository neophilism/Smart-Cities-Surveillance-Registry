# Policy, authorization, audit, and violation pages

PR 12 adds purpose-built accountability summaries for the registry's governance
and oversight record types.

Policy pages foreground authority level, citation, status, review date, issuing
agency, governed deployments, governed data-sharing arrangements, evidence, and
public deadlines.

Authorization pages foreground approval type, approving body, reference number,
conditions, status, effective/expiration dates, issuing agency, authorized
deployments, evidence, and expiration deadlines.

Audit pages foreground assessment type, assessor, review period, findings
status, recommendations, next review, affected deployments, conducting agency,
identified incidents, evidence, and follow-up deadlines.

Violation pages foreground incident type, status, severity, occurrence/report
dates, remediation, remediation due date, resolution date, affected deployment,
identifying audit, evidence, and remediation deadlines.

All summaries continue to consume only the public lifecycle/disclosure/safety
projection.
